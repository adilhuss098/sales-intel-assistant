LINKEDIN PROFILE:
Tom Ashworth
Co-Founder & CEO · Recast · London

About
Building Recast — we help DTC and e-commerce brands understand which marketing channels are actually driving revenue, using Bayesian MMM (media mix modelling). Think of it as an alternative to last-click attribution that doesn't break when iOS blocks tracking.

Raised a £2.1M pre-seed in Jan 2024 (led by Episode 1, Frontline). 8 people. First 20 customers are DTC brands doing £5M-£50M revenue. Closing first enterprise pilot with a major UK retailer.

Background: 5 years at Facebook (now Meta) as a measurement partner manager, working with retail and e-comm advertisers. Then 2 years at a small marketing analytics consultancy. Dropped out of that to start Recast in 2023.
